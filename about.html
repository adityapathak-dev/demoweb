About the Teacher

Meet the person behind Excel Academy — dedicated to transforming every student's potential into performance.

👨‍🏫

Rajendra Sharma

Founder & Lead Teacher, Excel Academy

With over 12 years of experience teaching Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, I founded Excel Academy with a simple belief — every student can score well if they're taught the right way.

I've worked with 500+ students across Classes 8 to 12, helping them prepare for CBSE boards, ICSE exams, and competitive tests like JEE and NEET. My focus has always been on building conceptual clarity rather than rote memorisation.

M.Sc. Mathematics B.Ed. 12+ Years Experience 500+ Students CBSE & ICSE Expert

💡 Teaching Philosophy

"I don't believe in making students memorise formulas. I believe in making them understand why a formula works. When a student truly understands a concept, they don't just pass exams — they develop a love for learning that stays with them for life."
